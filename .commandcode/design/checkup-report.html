// TL;DR

Untangle is a genuinely authored product: obsidian canvas, one solar-amber accent, an ADHD-first copy voice, and a dump → slice → focus composition that matches the work. Both surfaces carry it.

Web is blocked by an access layer that was never designed: three overlays with no dialog semantics, focus trap, Escape, or restore; controls focusable with nothing visible to show focus landed, or mouse-only; delete with no undo; a placeholder doing a label's job; looping motion ignoring reduced motion.

And the composition does not serve the work. At desktop width the first viewport is header, hero card with marketing copy, banner, composer with three pill rows, then two filter strips. The task list a user opened the app for only appears after scrolling past all of it. Logged as finding 21.

Native is much healthier. Its gaps: live regions are Android-only, web-only features are amputated, an offline banner styled as an error, a blank-screen edge on boot, a checkmark drawn inside a radio, and a placeholder app icon.

Run /design a11y first, scoped to web. It clears every escalation trigger at once.

MEDIUM · LAYOUT

The work is below the fold

MEDIUM · COLOR

The accent has stopped being an accent

Amber carries the active nav state, the Unstick Me action, the hero eyebrow, two section headers, every selection pill, and the primary submit. Measured on the full-page capture, pure amber is 0.18% of the page with two substantial fills, the largest 114×28 (the Unstick button).

FIX: Give amber one job, the single primary action per screen. Move selection state and section headings to neutral treatments. Role dilution, not area excess — the measurement rules out a coverage problem, so the earlier "accent should be ~10% of the surface" prescription was wrong. The cost is that one colour means selection, action and heading at once, so it signals nothing in particular.
