The score is unchanged, because it was already in the bottom band. The count of distinct tells went from 7 to 11: the first pass missed the hero imagery, the pill saturation, the uppercase micro-label reflex and the vendor name-dropping, and it understated the type problem by roughly half.

Dominant smell: the median generated dashboard. A centred column of equal rounded panels, one type size almost everywhere, unfocused depth applied as blur, and every concept given its own card, its own proper noun, and its own row of pills.

Root reflex: enumeration instead of composition. If there is a feature it gets a rounded panel and a label. The page substitutes listing for structure, which is why nothing is subordinate and there is no rhythm to find.

Counterweight, still true: the palette and the copy voice are real decisions. Obsidian plus one amber accent is not the domain's reflex, there is no blue-violet, and the no-guilt ADHD voice belongs to this product alone. Two of the seven decisions the catalog asks for are present; five default.

Recommended: /design redesign, not repair. Composition, type, depth and imagery default at once.
